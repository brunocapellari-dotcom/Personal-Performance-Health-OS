# Personal Performance & Health OS — GitHub Research Integration 2026-10-01

Status: APPROVED RESEARCH ADDENDUM

## Training Load & Performance Model Lab

GoldenCheetah is REFERENCE for established endurance/performance analytics concepts, not a code dependency by default.

Methods to evaluate against primary literature and our own data include:

- session RPE load;
- TRIMP-family load;
- critical power;
- W-prime / W' balance concepts;
- acute/chronic training-state representations;
- Banister/impulse-response concepts;
- performance management charts;
- workload, fatigue and recovery visualization.

No metric becomes a safety or training decision rule solely because another application implements it.

## Hybrid athlete adaptation

The system remains oriented to a hybrid/tactical-operational profile, not cycling-specific optimization.

Any endurance-derived metric must be tested for usefulness alongside:

- strength and relative strength;
- hypertrophy/functional mass;
- running/aerobic conditioning;
- anaerobic conditioning;
- muscular endurance;
- rucking/loaded movement;
- carries/drags/grip;
- mobility/robustness;
- recovery/readiness.

## UX/reference

GymMane and similar training applications are REFERENCE for useful logging/progression/UX patterns only. Do not fork a general fitness app into the core architecture.

## Evidence boundary

Research AI/Second Brain remain the scientific knowledge sources. Health OS stores evidence references and derived user state.

Training/readiness models are decision-support. They do not diagnose disease or independently alter medications.

## Shared engineering stack

Consume shared Hermes capabilities:

- Pydantic contracts;

- OpenTelemetry;
- Dagger/engineering factory where useful;
